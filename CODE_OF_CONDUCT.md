# Community Code of Conduct

OpenJControlTower welcomes contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Our community aims to be technically ambitious, inclusive and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, polite and professional in issues, reviews and discussions.
- Discuss technical approaches in good faith and support disagreements with evidence.
- Credit others' work and welcome thoughtful feedback.
- Protect community members' privacy and handle reported vulnerabilities responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, targeted insults, sexualized language or unwanted attention, threats, discrimination, publication of private information without permission and deliberate disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove abusive content, ask participants to stop, suspend access or permanently ban repeated offenders. Reports may be submitted confidentially to project maintainers using the repository's available private contact or platform reporting mechanisms. Please do not publicly disclose personally identifying details in a report.

Decisions should be proportionate, documented internally where appropriate and made fairly, with an opportunity to clarify misunderstandings.
